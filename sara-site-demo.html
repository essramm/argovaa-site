Procedures offered

Which approach fits, if any, is decided at a consultation. These are short descriptions of what each one is.

FUT

Follicular units are taken from a thin strip of donor scalp and placed individually.

Read more →

FUE

Follicular units are extracted one at a time from the donor area, without a linear strip.

Read more →

Limited shave FUE

An FUE approach that shaves only small sections of the donor area.

Read more →

Eyebrow restoration

Transplanting individual hairs to rebuild or reshape the brows.

Hairline feminization
